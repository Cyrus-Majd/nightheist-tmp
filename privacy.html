Privacy policy

Night Heist uploader · last updated 16 November 2026

What the app is

Night Heist uploader is a private tool used only by the Night Heist Clips channel to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has no other users.

What data it uses

  • OAuth access to the Night Heist Clips YouTube channel, with permission to upload videos and read the channel's basic details, so it can confirm it is posting to the right channel.
  • Access to the Night Heist Clips Instagram professional account, to publish Reels.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone else. Access tokens are stored only on the channel's own computer and are used only to publish the channel's videos.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions: message Night Heist Clips on Instagram or through the YouTube channel.
